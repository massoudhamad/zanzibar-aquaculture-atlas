What you will build

A map of surveyed aquaculture locations with activity colours, village search, district and activity filters, a result list and farm details. Every lab uses the same project, so your atlas grows as you learn.

Open the completed teaching atlas to see the goal. Internet is needed for Leaflet and the basemap.

Before you start

You need a browser, a text editor such as VS Code, and Python 3 or Live Server. Basic computer skills are enough to begin; practise variables, arrays and functions before loading the dataset in Lab 3.

Use the survey accurately

The class dataset has 437 location records linked to 301 survey responses. It primarily covers southern Unguja. Eleven activity labels are missing, ten records share coordinates, and one crab label needs verification. Names, contacts, photographs and submission identifiers are excluded.
